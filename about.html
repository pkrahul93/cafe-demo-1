The Suto story

A place made for everyday moments.

We wanted to create a café that feels familiar from the first visit — relaxed, welcoming and easy to come back to.

Suto brings together thoughtfully prepared food, comforting drinks and an atmosphere that works just as well for a solo coffee as it does for a table full of friends.

Our menu is designed around the things people actually crave: familiar favourites, satisfying meals, refreshing drinks and something sweet when the moment calls for it.

Located in New Rajdhani Enclave, Preet Vihar, Suto Cafe is your neighbourhood spot for good food and better company.
